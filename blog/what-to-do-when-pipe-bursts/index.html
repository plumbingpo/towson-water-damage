Steps to Take When a Pipe Bursts in Towson, MD

Home / Blog / Steps to Take When a Pipe Bursts in Towson, MD

A burst pipe is one of the most stressful plumbing emergencies a homeowner can experience. At 4 to 8 gallons every minute, one broken pipe can soak whole rooms, damage what you own, and compromise the structure in a matter of hours. Knowing exactly what to do in the first critical minutes can save you thousands of dollars in damage and separate a manageable repair from a major restoration project. Towson Restoration put together these steps so Towson, MD residents know exactly how to respond to this common problem.

Step 1: Turn the Water Off Right Away

Before anything else, cut off the water, because nothing you do afterward matters as much as stopping the flow quickly. The longer it runs, the more you lose and the more you pay. Here's what to do: locate your main water shutoff valve. In most Towson homes, it sits near the water meter, typically in the garage, basement, utility room, or along an exterior wall near the street. Turn the valve clockwise (righty-tighty) to shut off water flow to the entire house. If you can identify the specific pipe that burst and it has its own shutoff valve nearby, close that valve as well.

Your water company can cut the supply at the meter, so call them at once if the valve is missing or will not budge. It is also why every homeowner should learn where the shutoff is long before anything goes wrong. Take a few minutes today to locate yours and make sure it operates smoothly. Should the handle be seized, get a plumber out to fix it well ahead of any emergency.

Step 3: Protect Your Property and Belongings

While waiting for professional help, take these steps to minimize damage: move valuable items, electronics, and documents away from wet areas. Furniture resting on damp carpet should be raised, with foil or plastic blocks set under each leg. Take area rugs off floors that got wet. Mop up accessible standing water with towels. Prop cabinet and closet doors open so air can move through. Turn off electricity to affected areas at the breaker panel if you can do so safely without standing in water.

Do not step into pooled water anywhere close to outlets, electrical panels, or appliances. If the burst pipe has caused significant flooding and you cannot safely access your electrical panel, leave the electricity on and wait for professionals to assess the situation. No belonging is worth risking an injury.

Step 4: Take Photos Before You Clean Up

Your insurance claim will need evidence, so record everything carefully before any cleaning starts. Take photos and video of all standing water, damaged materials, and affected belongings. Note the date and approximate time you discovered the burst pipe, and document the source of the leak if visible. Towson Restoration supplies detailed reports as well, and paired with your notes they strengthen your claim and your case for fair payment.

Lowering Your Burst Pipe Risk in Towson

While not all pipe bursts can be prevented, Towson homeowners can significantly reduce their risk by insulating exposed pipes in unheated areas like garages, attics, and crawl spaces. During cold weather, keep faucets dripping slightly to maintain water movement. Warm room air should be able to reach pipes on exterior walls, so open the cabinets under those sinks. Pressure over 80 PSI calls for a pressure reducing valve, so check your reading from time to time. Swap old supply lines for braided stainless steel, starting with any rubber hoses on the washing machine. Above all, make sure you can find your main shutoff long before an emergency.
