Pipes that burst rank among the most frequent causes of water damage, and they can do devastating harm to a building. At 4 to 8 gallons a minute, just one burst can send 240 to 480 gallons each hour into your ceilings, walls and flooring. Pipes burst from freezing, old plumbing, too much water pressure, corrosion or a physical hit, and in every case a fast emergency response is what keeps water from ruining flooring, structural materials, building systems and your belongings.

Towson Restoration provides 24/7 emergency response for burst pipe water damage throughout Towson and surrounding areas. We can help find the shutoff and stop the water, and our team works with licensed plumbers to see that the pipe itself is repaired correctly. As soon as the flow is shut down, extraction starts: portable and truck-mounted units pull standing water out of every affected area as fast as we can.

After the water is out, we dry every material that took on moisture: finished floors, subflooring, wood framing, insulation and drywall. Gravity carries the water through walls, floors and ceilings, so damage from one burst commonly shows up in several rooms and on different floors. Hidden moisture leads to mold and a deteriorating structure, so we use moisture detection equipment and thermal imaging to map the water's path and make sure all affected areas get dried.

Why Hire a Professional for Burst Pipe Repair?

Burst pipe water damage often extends far beyond what is visible on the surface. Materials get saturated in spots that can stay undiscovered for days or weeks, because the water runs along conduits, joists, floor assemblies and the insides of walls. At that point mold can already be growing out of sight, and the structure may have lost a significant amount of strength. Our technicians find all the wet areas at the start by tracing migration with thermal imaging and meter readings.

Professional-grade extraction and drying equipment also works on a completely different scale than consumer tools. Compared with a shop vacuum, truck-mounted extraction is hundreds of times faster, and compared with a residential dehumidifier, a commercial one removes 10 to 20 times as much moisture. Mold can begin growing in 24 to 48 hours, so this kind of power and speed matters a great deal.

Maintenance Tips for Burst Pipe Repair

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Outdoor faucets should be closed, drained and free of hoses ahead of freezing weather
  • Know where your main water shutoff valve is and how to operate it quickly
  • Every few years, ask a licensed plumber to look over the system for corrosion and worn parts
  • Check water pressure with a gauge, since anything over 80 PSI puts stress on fittings and pipes
  • Open sink cabinets and leave a slow drip running on cold nights, which helps keep pipes from freezing
  • Going away in cold months? Keep the thermostat running to protect the plumbing from freezing

Warning Signs for Burst Pipe Repair

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Pipes coated in ice or frost when temperatures drop
  • No water at a tap in freezing weather, a sign the line is frozen
  • Stale or earthy smells coming from a cabinet or wall cavity
  • Brownish water at the faucet, which can mean corroded pipes
  • Exposed pipes that drip, mist or shoot water
  • Water pressure that falls off abruptly at every fixture
  • Puddles on the floor that you cannot trace to anything obvious
